# 07 · Teardown: Swolo (@swolotips)

**Researched 9 Nov 2026.** Sources:
- swolotips.com, which publishes Swolo's own platform numbers.
- Swolo's YouTube Shorts channel: titles, view counts, thumbnails, and frames sampled at 25%, 50% and 75% of each video.

Instagram requires a login, so Instagram and TikTok figures are Swolo's self-reported ones.

---

## 1. What Swolo is

| | |
|---|---|
| **Positioning** | "Fitness Explained in 60 Seconds". "One move a day. No gym, no gear." |
| **Core promise** | *"What actually happens to your body when you do one simple move every day for 30 days."* |
| **Character** | **"A very swole little green guy"**: a faceless, hyper-muscular 3D humanoid. Dark teal matte skin, a bean-shaped head, **two white oval eyes, no mouth, no nose**. Mint shorts, bare feet, mint background (`#B8DDD7`). |
| **Reach (self-reported)** | **293K Instagram · 147K TikTok · 21M views.** YouTube: **2,050 subscribers, 37 Shorts** |
| **Business** | They're building **an app**. Waitlist: *"You've watched the 30 days. Now do them."* It has hold timers, rep counts, streaks, and friend challenges. **This is a direct competitor to Elovia's workout side.** |
| **Website extras** | Free "how long can you hold it?" tests (wall sit, dead hang, plank, one-leg balance) that compare you with people your age. These capture leads and get shared. |

## 2. The video formula

```
TITLE   "What Happens If You Do [simple move] Every Day for 30 Days"
0–3s    Swolo in the pose + one big word on screen ("days?") + the question.
3–15s   WEEK 1: the struggle (shaking, pain highlighted in red, "you'll quit at 40 seconds").
15–35s  WEEK 2–3: what adapts. Anatomy cutaway (orange muscle overlay, bones),
        before/after side-by-side with measurement lines and arrows.
35–55s  WEEK 4: the payoff (posture line straighter, hold longer). Thumbs-up.
```

**What it looks like (seen in the frames):**
- Polished 3D renders, almost certainly AI-generated stills or short clips. The character design stays perfectly consistent.
- **One word of caption at a time**: huge, bold, condensed, dark teal, centred near the top ("your", "stop", "socket", "blades").
- A faint **"Week One / Two / Three"** printed large behind the character, so the timeline is always visible.
- **Anatomy cutaways** in a medical-illustration style: orange-red muscles, white bones, the shoulder socket, the scapula.
- **Annotation language**: red glow on pain points, orange arrows for movement direction, yellow measurement lines for posture comparisons, side-by-side "before / after".
- Ends on a thumbs-up or a proud pose. `@swolotips` watermark at the top.
- No dialogue and no lip sync. A **narrator speaks to "you"**, and the character acts it out.

## 3. What performs (YouTube Shorts, 37 videos)

| Rank | Video | Views |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hold Your Arms Out Every Day for 30 Days | **131,000** |
| 2 | Tib Raises Every Day for 30 Days | **49,000** |
| 3 | Wall Angels Every Day for 30 Days | **30,000** |
| 4 | Side Plank Every Day for 30 Days | 23,000 |
| 5 | Hang From a Bar for 30 Days | 13,000 |
| 6 | Wall Handstand for 30 Days | 10,000 |
| — | **Median video** | **about 3,000** |
| bottom | 8 Hours of Sleep (930), 3 Eggs (1,000), Dead Bugs (118) | |

**What the data says:**
1. **Weird-but-simple beats classic.** "Hold your arms out" (131K) and "tib raises" (49K) crush squats (4.7K) and push-ups (3K). The share trigger is: *"Wait, that's it? I can do that right now."*
2. **Posture and pain angles travel** (wall angels, side plank, chin tucks). People share them with the friend who hunches at a desk.
3. **The formula title beats clever titles.** "You're Sitting On It Right Now — 30 Days of Glute Bridges" got 1.3K; "You Quit at 40 Seconds — 30 Days of Planking" got 1.9K.
4. **Swolo is weak on nutrition and lifestyle** (eggs 1K, sleep 930). Food and habit topics aren't what this audience comes to Swolo for, which leaves an open lane for Elovia.
5. **YouTube is their weak platform** (2K subscribers, ~3K median views). Instagram is where they're big.

**Caveats:** YouTube is their smallest platform, so their Instagram winners may differ. One channel is one data point. The *mechanism* (simple move + curiosity + visible timeline + anatomy payoff) is what transfers, not the specific moves.

## 4. Teardown by layer: what's driving it

| Layer | Swolo | Tag |
|---|---|---|
| **Hook** | "What happens if you…" + a move you can do *right now* | **DRIVER** |
| **Emotion** | Curiosity, then aspiration (the swole body), then "I could do that" | **DRIVER** |
| **Retention** | The Week 1 → 4 timeline is an open loop; you stay to see Week 4 | **DRIVER** |
| **Format** | Faceless 3D hulk, one word on screen at a time, anatomy cutaways | Supporting (copyable, and copied) |
| **Share trigger** | Practical value ("try this"), plus sending posture videos to friends | **DRIVER** |
| **Character** | Faceless means anyone can project onto it; hyper-swole means aspiration | Supporting |
| **Distribution** | Daily posting; consistent template | Supporting |

**Mechanism in one line:** *a tiny, doable action, plus a promised 30-day reveal, explained with "inside your body" visuals.*

## 5. Where Swolo is weak, and how Elovia beats it

| Swolo's gap | Elovia's move |
|---|---|
| **One male hulk.** Women (a big share of fitness-app users) don't see themselves in it, and the body is unattainable for beginners | **Elo and Ela**: a male and a female character. Ela is toned, not bulky (see `02`) |
| **The character never changes.** It's huge on Day 1 and huge on Day 30 | **Level-up mechanic:** Elo and Ela *start* at Level 1 with an ordinary body and visibly improve. Viewers see themselves at Level 1 |
| **No personality and no recurring cast.** It works as an explainer, with nothing to get attached to | Named characters, signature moments, a rotating Food Gang, the Raja "hype" episodes |
| **No nutrition** (its food videos flop) | A whole lane Swolo isn't in: food truth, the 71-dish Indian food database, scan and log |
| **App not live** (waitlist only) | Elovia has real workouts, streaks, achievements, PRs, muscle progress and social challenges. Every video ends with a real screen recording |
| **English only, Western context** | Hinglish dubs and India-specific challenges (Surya Namaskar, uthak-baithak, the post-dinner walk, stairs in your building) |
| **Claims without sources** ("the research on blood pressure") | Show a **source line** on screen for every science claim, and keep the claims conservative |
| **Templated** (YouTube's "inauthentic content" policy risk) | A different visual set-up per episode, plus real app data and founder episodes |
| **Static stills with light motion** | Real motion (image-to-video) for the key move, with stills for the explainer beats |

**Don't copy:** the teal and mint palette, the faceless-hulk silhouette, the "@swolotips"-style
watermark, or their exact titles and wording. Elovia's look is **deep electric blue on charcoal with
cyan light**, in the app's palette. The *format* (30-day curiosity) is a genre and is fair to use;
the *character and art direction* are theirs.
