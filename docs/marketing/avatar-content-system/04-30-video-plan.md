# 04 · The 30-video plan (v2: "30 days" format with Elo and Ela)

**v2 (9 Nov 2026)** replaces the v1 food-scan plan, which is still in git history (commit `26cb882`).
It's built on the Swolo teardown (`07`): **same proven genre, better execution.**

**Window:** Mon 16 Nov to Tue 15 Dec 2026, one per day. Week 0 (10–15 Nov) is for building the
golden sheets, locking the voice and banking Days 1–7.

### What's different from Swolo

| Swolo | Elovia |
|---|---|
| One male hulk, maximum swole from Day 1 | **Elo and Ela** start at **Level 1** (an ordinary body) and reach **Level 2** by Day 30. The viewer sees themselves |
| No women's version | Ela gets about half the episodes: toned, not bulky |
| No nutrition | A **food and habit lane** (logging, protein, chai sugar, steps, water), which is Swolo's blind spot |
| Western moves only | **India specials**: murga pose, uthak-baithak, Surya Namaskar, the post-dinner walk, taking the stairs |
| Watch only | **Try-it-now tests** ("comment your score") plus a real app screen in every video |
| Claims without sources | A **source line** on every science claim, and honest payoffs ("no six-pack in 30 days") |

### Format rules (every episode)

- **Title formula:** `What Happens If You [simple action] Every Day for 30 Days`. In Swolo's data the formula beat clever titles.
- **Structure:** HOOK (Level 1 + one word "30 days?") → WEEK 1 (struggle) → WEEK 2 (adaptation + anatomy overlay) → WEEK 4 (Level 2 + honest payoff) → app moment → loop.
- **Length:** 20–35s. **Narrator voice** speaks to "you"; Elo and Ela don't talk.
- **Template:** `marketing/video-studio` (Remotion), with the episode JSON per `08`.
- **Science lines:** cite only real, checked sources. The sources named below are believed accurate but **must be verified before publishing**. With no source, make no claim; describe the experience instead ("your legs shake").
- **Market tags:** 🇮🇳 IN = Hinglish/India only · 🌍 BOTH = render the EN and Hinglish voice and captions from the same visuals.
- **Totals:** Elo 12 · Ela 11 · Elo & Ela duo 3 · Raja 3 · founder 1. 7 IN-only, 23 BOTH. That's 30 posts on the India account and 23 on the US account.

## Week 1 · Launch the format (highest-confidence moves)

**D01 · Mon 16 Nov · ELO · 🌍 · Wall sit**
- *Hook:* Level 1 Elo slides down a wall, eyes exhausted. On screen: **"30 days?"** (A demo render already exists: `characters/demo-elo-wall-sit.mp4`.)
- *W1:* legs shake at 30s · *W2:* quads and glutes adapt (anatomy overlay) · *W4:* a full minute feels doable, stairs feel easier.
- *Payoff:* "No six-pack. Stronger legs? Yes." Level 1 vs Level 2 side by side.
- *Source:* isometric exercise such as wall sits lowered blood pressure in a 2023 network meta-analysis (Edwards et al., *Br J Sports Med*). Verify.
- *Comment bait:* "How long can you hold one right now?"

**D02 · Tue 17 Nov · ELA · 🌍 · Can't do one push-up? Wall push-ups → real push-up**
- *Hook:* Ela tries a floor push-up and face-plants softly; eyes in "shocked" shape. **"0 push-ups?"**
- *W1:* wall push-ups · *W2:* kitchen-counter incline · *W4:* her first full push-up (knees down is fine too).
- *Anatomy:* chest, triceps, front delts.
- *Payoff:* "Progress isn't a number. It's the angle getting lower."
- *App moment:* rep logging, then **PR confetti** (shipped feature).

**D03 · Wed 18 Nov · ELO · 🌍 · 10-minute walk after dinner**
- *Hook:* Level 1 Elo on the sofa with a full plate. **"10 minutes?"**
- *W1:* "feels pointless" · *W2:* a habit forms, family joins (an India post-dinner-walk vibe) · *W4:* a better sleep routine and more steps.
- *Source:* light walking after meals blunted blood-sugar rises vs sitting (Buffey et al., *Sports Medicine*, 2022). Verify. No diabetes claims.
- *App moment:* the step count on Home (Apple Health / Health Connect sync).

**D04 · Thu 19 Nov · ELA · 🌍 · Stand up from the floor without hands** *(a test episode)*
- *Hook:* Ela sitting cross-legged. **"Can you stand up without your hands?"**
- *Beat:* how the sit-to-rise test is scored (start at 10, minus 1 for each hand, knee or support used).
- *Source:* sit-to-rise test scores were associated with all-cause mortality in older adults (Brito et al., *Eur J Prev Cardiol*, 2012). Verify, and present it as an association, not a prediction.
- *Comment bait:* "**Comment your score /10.**" Expect a big comment section.

**D05 · Fri 20 Nov · ELO · 🇮🇳 · Murga pose (the school punishment)**
- *Hook:* Elo in a tiny school uniform tie, in murga pose. **"School punishment… ya leg day?"** ("…or leg day?")
- *W1:* thighs burn, nostalgia · *W2:* quads, glutes and hamstrings endurance (anatomy) · *W4:* holds longer, comfortably.
- *Guardrail:* "Knee or back pain? Skip it." Keep holds short. Make it comedy plus nostalgia, not a promise.
- *Comment bait:* "Tag the friend who was always in murga."

**D06 · Sat 21 Nov · ELA · 🌍 · Take the stairs every day**
- *Hook:* Ela stares at a lift with a "Lift out of order" sign. **"30 days of stairs?"**
- *W1:* out of breath at floor 3 · *W2:* calves and glutes adapt · *W4:* floor 5 without stopping.
- *App moment:* steps and flights in the activity summary (show only what the app actually displays).

**D07 · Sun 22 Nov · RAJA MODE #1 · 🌍 · "Pick your 30-day challenge"**
- *Hook:* Raja walks out of the dark, golden light on him. **"This is you. At Level 3."**
- *Beat:* Raja shows three challenges (wall sit, push-ups, walking); Elo and Ela appear at Level 1 beside him.
- *CTA:* "Comment WALL, PUSH or WALK. Start it in Elovia with a friend" (Elovia has **social challenges**).
- *Why:* turns viewers into participants; seeds the comment-reply videos.

## Week 2 · Open the food and habit lane, India specials

**D08 · Mon 23 Nov · ELO · 🌍 · Log everything you eat for 30 days**
- *Hook:* Elo's eyes do **the Scan** on a plate; a calorie card pops up. **"Every meal?"**
- *W1:* "I eat THAT much?" · *W2:* awareness changes choices · *W4:* it takes 2 minutes a day.
- *Source:* self-monitoring of diet is consistently associated with weight-loss success (Burke et al., *J Am Diet Assoc*, 2011, systematic review). Verify.
- *App moment:* food search and logging (free) or the AI photo scan (Pro; never call it free).

**D09 · Tue 24 Nov · ELA · 🇮🇳 · 12 Surya Namaskars every day**
- *Hook:* Ela at sunrise on a rooftop. **"12 rounds? Daily?"**
- *W1:* hamstrings tight, breath fast · *W2:* the flow gets smoother · *W4:* touches the floor, rhythm and breath in sync.
- *Anatomy:* posterior chain, shoulders, hip flexors. No spiritual or medical claims.

**D10 · Wed 25 Nov · ELO · 🌍 · Carry your groceries (farmer's carry)**
- *Hook:* Elo with two heavy shopping bags, eyes in "exhausted" shape. **"Grip test."**
- *W1:* forearms burn · *W2:* traps and core brace (anatomy) · *W4:* one trip, not two.
- *Source:* lower grip strength was associated with higher mortality across 17 countries (Leong et al., *Lancet*, 2015, the PURE study). Verify, and present it as an association.

**D11 · Thu 26 Nov · ELA · 🌍 · 7,000 steps every day** *(US Thanksgiving day; the walk-after-the-feast angle)*

- *Source:* about 7,000 steps a day was associated with substantially lower all-cause mortality vs 2,000 (Ding et al., *Lancet Public Health*, 2025). Verify.

**D12 · Fri 27 Nov · ELO · 🇮🇳 · Uthak-baithak (ear-hold squats)**

**D13 · Sat 28 Nov · ELA · 🌍 · Bear plank hold**
- *Hook:* Ela on all fours, knees hovering an inch, shaking. **"An inch. That's it."**
- *W1:* shaking at 20s · *W2:* deep core and shoulders (anatomy) · *W4:* 60s, steady.

**D14 · Sun 29 Nov · ELO & ELA · 🌍 · TEST: "How long can you wall sit?"**

- *Benchmarks:* say "most beginners…", not made-up age norms.

## Week 3 · Trust and depth

**D15 · Mon 30 Nov · ELO · 🇮🇳 · 100g paneer every day**
- *Hook:* the Scan on a paneer block: **~265 kcal · ~18g protein** (app numbers). **"Protein problem solved?"**
- *W1:* hitting protein feels easier · *W2:* fuller between meals (keep it experiential) · *W4:* the macro bar is finally green.
- *App moment:* the protein macro bar. Use the user's own target; don't quote one target for everyone.

**D16 · Tue 1 Dec · ELA · 🌍 · Doorway chest stretch (for desk workers)**
- *Hook:* Ela hunched over a laptop, posture line in red. **"Desk job?"**
- *W1:* tight chest · *W2:* shoulders sit back (yellow posture line) · *W4:* standing taller in photos.
- *Comment bait:* "Send this to your most hunched coworker."

**D17 · Wed 2 Dec · ELO · 🌍 · Nordic curls under your bed**
- *Hook:* Elo hooks his feet under a bed. **"Your bed is a gym."**
- *W1:* "I fell after 2 inches" · *W2:* hamstrings (anatomy) · *W4:* a controlled lower halfway.
- *Guardrail:* lower slowly, use your hands, and make it a progression, not a max effort.

**D18 · Thu 3 Dec · ELA · 🌍 · 3 litres of water a day: what changes and what doesn't**
- *Hook:* Ela with a giant bottle, eyes in "shocked" shape. **"3 litres?"**
- *Honest payoff:* "Water won't melt fat. It helps when it replaces sugary drinks." Myth-busting builds trust.
- *App moment:* the hydration tracker filling up.

**D19 · Fri 4 Dec · ELO · 🇮🇳 · No sugar in your chai for 30 days**
- *Hook:* 4 chai cups, with the Scan on the sugar spoon. **"Sirf 2 chammach?"** ("Only 2 spoons?")
- *Maths (on screen):* 2 tsp ≈ 8g ≈ ~32 kcal per cup × 4 cups ≈ ~128 kcal a day ≈ **~3,800 kcal a month**.
- *W1:* chai tastes weird · *W2:* you notice the tea leaves · *W4:* sweet chai now tastes like syrup.

**D20 · Sat 5 Dec · FOUNDER (real) · 🌍 · "We built Elo because Day 1 is hard"**
- A real hand on a real phone: Elo's Level 1 sheet, then the real Elovia app: workouts, streaks, PRs.
- Be open: *"Elo is an AI-animated character. The app and the numbers are real."*
- CTA per launch status (see `06`).

**D21 · Sun 6 Dec · RAJA MODE #2 · 🌍 · "Day 15 is where everyone quits"**

- *Beat:* the midpoint dip is normal; lower the bar, don't quit; "a 2-minute version still counts."
