**Why India first:** Elovia's 71-dish Indian food database is a real edge over US-built calorie
apps, Hinglish comedy is where the founder has an unfair advantage, and fitness is among India's
most-engaged Reels genres. The US account comes almost free, because most videos are dubbed.
Revisit this after 30 days using installs, not views.

  includes cartoon and animated styles. Unlabelled AI content can be restricted or removed.

- [ ] **Bio** (IN): `Elo ⚡ AI character jo tumhari plate scan karta hai. Judge plate ko, tumhe nahi. Not a doctor. 👇 Elovia`

## 2. Posting rhythm

- **One video a day** on the India account and about four a week on the US account, following the plan.
- **Times to test in Week 1:** India 1–2 pm and 8–10 pm IST; US 6–9 pm ET. Pick a slot by Week 2
  based on your own data. Consistency matters more than the exact minute.
- **Upload natively** to each platform, on the same day. No cross-platform watermarks.
- **Slideshow lane (2–3 a week, extra):** turn each top video into a 4–6 slide carousel or TikTok
  photo post: hook slide → Elo scanning → the number → the swap → "save this". In one vendor's
  sample of breakout calorie-tracker posts, about 77% were slideshows. That's directional, but they
  cost minutes to make. Use the same Elo stills and the Scan Card template.

### First-hour routine (15–30 minutes, every post)

1. Post a pinned comment that asks the video's question ("Team idli or team paratha?").
2. Reply to the first 20–30 comments in Elo's voice: short, funny, never mean.
3. Save every food someone mentions to the **comment bank** (tracker tab). That's what feeds
   "You Asked, Elo Scanned".
4. Reply **with a video** to the best comment whenever you have a fitting clip.

## 3. Measurement

Instagram has said publicly that **watch time, likes per reach and sends per reach** are its top
ranking signals, and that **sends (shares)** matter most for reaching non-followers. Optimise for
shares and saves, not likes.

### Tracker (one row per post)

| Column | Where it comes from |
|---|---|
| Day / title / series / market / hook pattern | Plan |
| Views | Platform insights |
| Hold rate (share of viewers past ~3s; "swiped away" on YouTube) | Insights |
| Avg % watched / rewatches | Insights |
| **Shares per 1K views** | Calculated |
| **Saves per 1K views** | Calculated |
| Comments per 1K views | Calculated |
| Follows per 1K views | Insights |
| Profile visits → link taps | Insights |
| Installs (campaign link) | App Store Connect / Play Console |
| Notes (what went right or wrong) | You |

**There's no universal "good" number.** Judge every video against **your own running median**.
Above median on shares or saves means a winner; below median on hold rate means fix the hook.

### Review points (built into the plan)

- **Day 10:** rank the series by (shares + saves) per 1K views. Give the top series two more
  episodes and replace the weakest planned video. Check which **hook patterns** held best.
- **Day 20:** compare IN and US performance on the 🌍 BOTH videos (same visuals, different market).
  Decide where the next month's effort goes.
- **Day 30:** season review. Keep the top 3 series, cut the rest, and plan Season 2 for January,
  the biggest month for new fitness goals.

### Attribution (views are not installs)

- **App Store:** use App Store Connect **campaign links** (provider and campaign tokens) for each
  account, and read installs by source in App Analytics.
- **Google Play:** add a UTM `referrer` to the Play link for each account.
- **Recommended product change (not implemented):** add an optional *"How did you hear about
  Elovia?"* question to onboarding (Instagram / YouTube / TikTok / friend / other). It's the
  cheapest attribution there is for organic social, where links are tapped from a bio.

## 4. Phase 2 (after Day 30, once something's working)

1. **Boost the winners.** Put a small budget behind the top 2–3 organic videos (Spark Ads on
   TikTok, Reels boosts). Paid AI content must carry AI labels on every platform. In India,
   **ASCI's AI-labelling guidelines for ads take effect around 29 Dec 2026**.
2. **Add real creators (Cal AI's lesson).** Elo brings attention; real people scanning real plates
   bring installs. Brief 5–10 micro-creators (Indian food, gym, office-life niches) to scan *their*
   plate with Elovia, with Elo appearing in their video as an overlay or a reaction. Always disclose
   as "Paid partnership" or "#ad". **Results claims must be the creator's real, typical experience.**
3. **Bring in a real expert.** For any technical nutrition topic, collaborate with a **qualified
   dietitian, shown on screen with their credentials**. Elo can't be the expert.

## 5. Compliance: the pre-publish gate

**Not legal advice.** This is a practical checklist based on the rules found in the Nov 2026
research. Have a lawyer review the approach before running paid ads.

### Every video must pass

- [ ] Platform **AI label** toggled on
- [ ] **"AI character"** tag on screen; disclosure line in the caption
- [ ] Every calorie number **checked in the Elovia app**, shown with "~" and the portion
- [ ] **No** weight-loss promises ("lose X kg in Y days"), no disease claims (diabetes, PCOS,
  "cure", "reverse"), no "detox", no GLP-1 or weight-loss-injection content
- [ ] **No** AI testimonials and no results claims by Elo or any AI character
- [ ] **No** body shaming in words or visuals
- [ ] **No** real brand logos or trademarks; no parody of trademarked shows or formats
- [ ] Music from the platform's commercial library, or original audio
- [ ] Every app feature shown **exists in the current build** (scan, barcode, logging, PR
  confetti, muscle progress, steps sync, GPS runs)
- [ ] CTA matches launch status; price and trial wording is accurate for that storefront
- [ ] Nothing implies the **AI photo scan is free**. It's Pro (and in the trial); search, barcode and manual logging are free.
- [ ] Elo is on-model (compared with the golden sheet every 10th video)
- [ ] Captions proofread; flashes at most 3 per second
- [ ] Commenter handles shown on screen only through the platform's reply feature, or with permission

### The rules behind the checklist

| Where | Rule | What it means for Elo |
|---|---|---|
| 🇮🇳 India | **IT Rules amendments on synthetically generated information** (notified 10 Feb 2026, in force 20 Feb 2026) | These cover AI content that *appears real*. A clearly cartoon Elo arguably falls outside them, but **label anyway**. Platforms must get a declaration from you and label confirmed synthetic content. |
| 🇮🇳 India | **ASCI influencer guidelines** | Virtual influencers must disclose upfront that they're **not human**. |
| 🇮🇳 India | **ASCI AI-labelling guidelines** (final 29 Sep 2026, in force about 29 Dec 2026) | Synthetic brand ambassadors must be labelled. Paid AI recommendations need "Sponsored by [Brand]". A label doesn't make a misleading ad compliant. |
| 🇮🇳 India | **Consumer Affairs health-influencer guidelines (2023)**; **Drugs and Magic Remedies Act** | No diagnose, cure or treat claims. The Act's schedule lists obesity, so no "cure obesity" style claims. |
| 🇮🇳 India | **CDSCO advisory (Mar 2026)** | No direct or indirect promotion of weight-loss drugs. |
| 🇺🇸 US | **FTC fake reviews and testimonials rule** (in force since Oct 2024) | Testimonials from people who don't exist, including AI ones, are banned (about $53K per violation). |
| 🇺🇸 US | **FTC Endorsement Guides** | Virtual influencers count as endorsers; the brand is responsible for disclosure. An avatar can't claim personal results. |
| 🇺🇸 US | **New York synthetic-performer ad law** (in force Jun 2026) | Paid ads using synthetic performers need a conspicuous disclosure. |
| Instagram | **"AI-generated profile" label** (since 31 Aug 2026); "AI info" labels for realistic AI audio/video | Unlabelled AI-persona profiles lose recommendation reach. A realistic-sounding AI voice probably needs the label too. |
| YouTube | **Inauthentic-content policy** (Jul 2025, clarified Jul 2026); automatic AI labels (May 2026) | Mass-produced, templated videos lose monetisation; so do **AI personas posing as health experts**. Every episode needs a distinct script and real value. |
| TikTok | **AI labelling**, which covers cartoon and animated styles; users can turn down how much AI content they see | Label everything. Expect a lower reach ceiling for AI content on US TikTok than on Instagram or YouTube. |

Sources for these rules are listed in `01-what-is-working-now.md`.
