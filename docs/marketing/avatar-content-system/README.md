# Elovia × Elo: AI-avatar short-form content system

A complete, repeatable system for promoting Elovia with a recurring AI character on Instagram
Reels, YouTube Shorts and TikTok. Researched 9 Nov 2026.

## TL;DR

1. **The avatar: Elo.** A small, stylised 3D electric-blue mascot that grew out of the Elovia
   logo spark. Elo **lives inside the app** and scans your food. Honest about the number, never
   judges your body. Supported by a small cast of villain foods (Samosa Bhai, the Jamun Twins,
   2AM Noodles…) and body parts (Glutes, Liver).
2. **Why not a realistic AI human:** talking-food and character formats are what's working now,
   and **no calorie app has claimed them**. Hyper-real AI people are losing engagement once
   audiences notice, getting removed, and losing reach or monetisation under 2026 platform rules
   (Instagram's AI-profile label, YouTube's AI-health-expert policy, India's IT Rules and ASCI, the
   US FTC). A cartoon that's openly AI has no "reveal" to lose engagement over.
3. **How to beat the account you saw:** make the product the plot. Every video resolves with
   **Elo's scan**, a calorie card that *is* the real Elovia result, followed by 1–2 seconds of real
   app footage. Most viral AI-character accounts get views but no installs because the product
   isn't in the story.
4. **The plan:** 30 videos from **16 Nov to 15 Dec 2026**, covering leftover Diwali mithai,
   wedding season and US Thanksgiving, and ending with "start in December, not January". Nine
   recurring series. **30 productions become about 44 posts**, because 14 videos are dubbed into
   both Hinglish and English.
5. **Markets:** India first (Hinglish, Instagram + YouTube Shorts), with the US/global account
   (English, TikTok + Instagram + YouTube) fed mostly by dubs. Elovia's 71-dish Indian food
   database is an edge over US calorie apps.
6. **Cost and time:** about **$250–800 a month** in tools and about **14 hours a week** for 7
   videos once you're up to speed.

## Ground rules (non-negotiable)

- Calorie numbers come from **the Elovia app**, shown with "~" and the portion.
- Elo roasts **food and habits, never bodies**. Elo is **not a doctor** and makes no results claims.
- **Label everything as AI.** Real app footage in every video.
- Show only features that are **actually shipped**. The **AI photo scan is Pro**; search, barcode and manual logging are free. Never say "scan for free".
